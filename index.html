I. Academic & Professional Overview

David Terrence Giberti is a Staff Design Verification Engineer and Team Lead at Qualcomm Incorporated in Austin, Texas, with over fourteen years of industrial hardware engineering experience spanning processor microarchitecture, pre-silicon design verification, C++ DPI-C co-simulation infrastructure, and post-silicon microprocessor bring-up. In his current appointment within Qualcomm's Adreno GPU Shader Processor organization, he directs, manages, guides, and mentors a team of verification engineers across four products in the Qualcomm lineup (operating at Senior Staff scope). He has architected and built multiple GPU microarchitecture verification testbenches from scratch—covering the Multi-Hop Raytracer Unit / Ray Tracing Tree Traversal Unit (RTTU), Ray Tracing Unit (RTU), Tree Traversal Unit (TTU), L0 Cache, and Matrix Arithmetic Logic Unit (MALU — AI/ML Processor)—and engineered the SystemVerilog testbench infrastructure, DPI-C++ interface calls, and DPI-C++ glue logic that transform untimed calculation outputs from repository C++ functional and performance models (Ray Intersection, Ray Traversal, and Matrix Results) into cycle-accurate transactions for RTL verification.

Prior to joining Qualcomm, Mr. Giberti held engineering appointments at Advanced Micro Devices (AMD) as a Member of Technical Staff conducting x86/x64 post-silicon verification, hardware emulation, and C++/assembly diagnostic development for flagship EPYC and Ryzen CCX (CPU + Memory Hierarchy) processors; Cirrus Logic, where he served as primary verification owner across 11 design blocks for mixed-signal Audio Codecs; the Samsung Austin R&D Center (SARC | ACL), where he performed pre-silicon SystemVerilog/UVM verification on Exynos CPU Load-Store, Page Table-Walker, L1 Cache, and TLB MMU subsystems; and Arm Ltd., where he verified Cortex-A57, A72, A76, Ares, Zeus, Hera, and Hercules CPU core frontends and engineered automated Jenkins CI/CD synthesis and place-and-route timing pipelines. He holds a B.S. in Electrical Engineering from UCLA, an M.S. in Electrical Engineering from San José State University, a Graduate Certificate in AI/ML from UT Austin, is completing a Certificate in Silicon Chip Design at UC Santa Cruz Extension, and is pursuing an M.S. in Computer Science (Computing Systems) at the Georgia Institute of Technology.
