Directs, manages, guides, and mentors a team of verification engineers across four GPU products in the Qualcomm Snapdragon lineup (operating at Senior Staff scope), responsible for pre-silicon verification of hardware-accelerated ray tracing, cache, and matrix compute subsystems—specifically the Multi-Hop Raytracer Unit / Ray Tracing Tree Traversal Unit (RTTU), Ray Tracing Unit (RTU), Tree Traversal Unit (TTU), L0 Cache, and Matrix Arithmetic Logic Unit (MALU)—while architecting the SystemVerilog testbench infrastructure, C++ Direct Programming Interface (DPI-C) calls, and cycle-accurate DPI-C++ glue logic that bridge RTL simulation environments with repository-based C++ functional and high-performance reference models.

Industrial practice is complemented by graduate research and coursework in cycle-accurate computer architecture simulation (C++ SESC), dynamic out-of-order hardware design (SystemVerilog Tomasulo RTL), formal verification (Synopsys VC-Formal), distributed operating systems, and machine learning across the Georgia Institute of Technology, San José State University, the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA), the University of California, Santa Cruz, and The University of Texas at Austin, providing a rigorous foundation for SoC Performance Architecture, CPU/GPU Performance Modeling, and Microarchitecture.
